Frequently Asked Questions

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do you provide repiping services?

TaylorMade Plumbing provides water and drain repiping services for residential projects. The appropriate approach depends on the existing system, the condition of the piping and the scope of the project.

Do you work on water heaters?

Yes. TaylorMade Plumbing provides water heater service, including repair, replacement and installation support. Contact the company to discuss the specific water heater and service needed.

Do you provide drain services?

Drain-related service may include troubleshooting, cleaning and repair. Contact TaylorMade Plumbing to explain the symptoms and determine the appropriate service.

Do you provide remodel plumbing?

TaylorMade Plumbing provides residential plumbing support for kitchen, bathroom and other remodeling projects. Larger projects should be discussed in advance so the scope and scheduling requirements can be understood.

What areas do you serve?

TaylorMade Plumbing is based in St. Petersburg and serves homeowners in the surrounding Tampa Bay area. Contact the company to confirm service availability for your location.
