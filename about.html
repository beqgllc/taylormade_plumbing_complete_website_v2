Our Story

PLUMBING DONE THE RIGHT WAY

TaylorMade Plumbing LLC is a Florida limited liability company based in St. Petersburg and managed by Michael T. Taylor. Public business listings describe the company as a local plumbing service offering repairs, replacements, repiping, water heaters, fixtures, gas-line work and remodel plumbing.

We are known for our responsiveness, honesty, workmanship and clean job sites. We are primarily do work in St. Petersburg, however we also serve many other Tampa Bay-area communities.
