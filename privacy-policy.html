1. Introduction

TaylorMade Plumbing LLC (“TaylorMade Plumbing,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides residential plumbing services in St. Petersburg, Florida and the surrounding Tampa Bay area, including water heater repair and installation, fixture repair and replacement, re-piping, gas line work, drain services, and remodel plumbing support. TaylorMade Plumbing is a Florida Certified Plumbing Contractor (License No. CFC1432798).

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website at taylormadeplumbing.vercel.app (the “Website”), when you call, text, or email us, when you request a quote or schedule an appointment, or when you otherwise interact with TaylorMade Plumbing in connection with our services (collectively, the “Services”).

By using the Website or our Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use the Website or submit personal information to us — you're always welcome to reach us by phone instead.

2. Definitions

  • “Personal Information” means information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with you, such as your name, phone number, email address, or service address.
  • “Services” means the plumbing services, estimates, scheduling, and related communications TaylorMade Plumbing provides.
  • “Website” means taylormadeplumbing.vercel.app and any successor domain we operate.
  • “You” or “Your” means the person visiting the Website or requesting Services.

3. Scope of This Policy

This Policy covers information collected:

  • Through our Website, including the Contact and Schedule Service pages;
  • When you call, text, or email us directly;
  • When you request an estimate, book an appointment, or become a customer; and
  • From third parties, such as publicly posted customer reviews.

Our Services are intended for consumers located in Florida, and the Website is not directed to individuals located outside the United States. This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, apps, or platforms we don't own or control — even ones we link to or use to power part of our Services (for example, a scheduling, payment, or review platform). Review those providers' own privacy policies separately.

4. Information We Collect

4.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use the Contact or Schedule Service forms, call our office, or otherwise reach out, we may collect:

  • Contact details — first and last name, phone number, and email address (where provided);
  • Service details — the type of service requested (water heater, fixture repair/replacement, re-piping, gas line, drain service, remodel plumbing, leak/other), a description of the issue, and any additional details you provide;
  • Property information — the service address where work is requested, which may differ from your billing or mailing address;
  • Scheduling preferences — preferred appointment date and time window; and
  • Anything else you choose to share in a message, call, text, or email, such as access instructions or billing questions.

If you become a customer, we also collect what's needed to complete and invoice the work, such as a billing address. If online or card-on-file payment is offered through the Website or over the phone, card information is processed directly by our third-party payment processor and is not stored on TaylorMade Plumbing's own servers.

4.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, our hosting provider may automatically log certain technical information, including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Browser type/version, device type, and operating system;
  • Referring/exit pages, pages viewed, and links clicked; and
  • Date/time stamps and basic diagnostic or error data.

We use this to operate, secure, and maintain the Website. It is not linked to your identity unless you've also submitted one of our forms.

4.3 Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Website currently uses only the cookies or local storage strictly necessary for it to function (for example, remembering form input as you complete a page). If we later add analytics, advertising, or similar tracking technologies, we will update this section to describe them, their purpose, and your choices. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings; doing so will not prevent you from using our service request forms.

4.4 Information from Other Sources

We may also receive information from:

  • Public reviews — customer reviews posted on platforms such as Google, which we may reference or excerpt on our Reviews page;
  • Referrals — if an existing customer refers you and shares your contact information; and
  • Third-party scheduling or lead platforms, if you ever request service through one that connects consumers with local contractors.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes or estimates;
  • Schedule, confirm, reschedule, and dispatch service appointments;
  • Perform the plumbing work you request, including communicating with you before, during, and after a job;
  • Bill for and process payment for completed services;
  • Maintain service records, warranty information, and job history;
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Website;
  • Meet licensing, permitting, tax, and other legal obligations that apply to a Florida-licensed plumbing contractor;
  • Protect against fraud, unauthorized transactions, or other liability; and
  • With your consent, send appointment reminders or, where you've separately opted in, promotional messages about our services.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use what you submit through our forms for purposes unrelated to providing plumbing services, except as described here.

6. Text Messages, Calls, and Other Communications

If you give us a phone number, we may contact you by phone or text about matters tied directly to your request — appointment confirmations, technician arrival windows, invoices, or follow-up on completed work (“Transactional Messages”). Providing your number is your consent to receive Transactional Messages related to that request.

If we ever send promotional or marketing texts or calls (for example, seasonal maintenance reminders or special offers), we will only send them to numbers that gave prior express written consent to marketing messages specifically — separate from consent to Transactional Messages. You can opt out of marketing texts at any time by replying STOP, and opt out of marketing calls by telling our staff directly or contacting us using Section 16. Message and data rates may apply, and frequency may vary. Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile phone number or text-messaging opt-in consent data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

7. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers and vendors performing functions on our behalf — website hosting (the Website is hosted on Vercel), scheduling/CRM software, payment processing, accounting/invoicing, and business communication or texting platforms. These providers may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • Subcontractors or licensed technicians, where necessary to complete the work you've requested.
  • Permitting and regulatory authorities, such as local building departments, for permitted plumbing work.
  • Professional advisors — accountants, insurers, or attorneys — where necessary for our business operations.
  • Law enforcement or government authorities, where required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TaylorMade Plumbing, our customers, or others.
  • A successor entity, if TaylorMade Plumbing is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Complete an active service request or job;
  • Honor warranty obligations on completed work;
  • Satisfy Florida recordkeeping requirements for licensed contractors (permitting, invoicing, tax records); and
  • Resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or meet other legal obligations.

Once information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction, consistent with our obligations under the Florida Information Protection Act (Fla. Stat. § 501.171). This includes encrypted transmission of Website form submissions, restricted access to customer and scheduling data, and use of reputable, security-conscious vendors.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

TaylorMade Plumbing is a small, locally owned Florida contractor. Based on our size and operations, we do not currently meet the applicability thresholds under comprehensive privacy statutes such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or the Florida Digital Bill of Rights (FDBR) — both generally aimed at much larger companies (broadly, $25M+ or $1B+ in annual revenue, respectively, among other criteria). That said, we're glad to honor reasonable requests from anyone who contacts us. You may ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you in connection with your service request or inquiry;
  • Correct inaccurate contact or property information;
  • Delete information we no longer need, subject to our recordkeeping obligations as a licensed contractor; and
  • Stop marketing communications at any time (see Section 6).

You may also decline to provide certain information, understanding that this may limit our ability to schedule or complete requested work. To exercise any of these choices, contact us using Section 16. We may need to verify your identity or service history before completing a request.

11. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” (DNT) signal, and some states recognize the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as an opt-out preference. The Website does not currently sell personal information or use third-party targeted advertising that would trigger a response to these signals, so we do not currently process DNT/GPC signals differently from any other visit. If that changes — for example, if we add analytics or advertising tools — we will update this section and configure the Website to honor those signals where legally required.

12. Children's Privacy

The Website and Services are directed to adults seeking residential plumbing services and are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using Section 16 and we will take steps to delete it.

13. Third-Party Websites and Content

The Website may link to third-party sites — such as Google Reviews, social media pages, or mapping services — or display publicly available reviews. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Review their policies separately before providing information to them.

14. Florida Data Breach Notification

In accordance with the Florida Information Protection Act (Fla. Stat. § 501.171), if we experience a data breach involving your personal information, we will notify you in writing within 30 days of determining a breach occurred, unless a longer period is needed for a law enforcement investigation or to determine the breach's scope. If a breach affects 500 or more Florida residents, we will also notify the Florida Department of Legal Affairs within the same timeframe.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, Website functionality, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Please check back periodically.
